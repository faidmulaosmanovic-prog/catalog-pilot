# Product Hunt timing

**Decision (31 October 2026): do not schedule a Product Hunt launch.**

The go-to-market plan says to launch only after the product is stable for 10 or more external users, and to treat Product Hunt as awareness rather than the way the first customers are found. The scale gate is separate: at least 3 paid pilots using the product weekly.

Neither count is recorded yet. Scheduling a date now would invent a launch that the gates do not allow.

## Revisit when both are true

1. At least 10 external users have completed an import and an export on their own organization.
2. At least 3 pilots are paying and using the product in a given week.

Until then, acquisition stays on the outreach tracker in [PILOT_OUTREACH.md](./PILOT_OUTREACH.md).
