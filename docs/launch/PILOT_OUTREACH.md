# Pilot outreach

CatalogPilot does not store a list of real prospects in this repository. Fill the tracker below as you contact people. Do not invent names, emails, or replies.

Weekly rhythm from the go-to-market plan:

- About 50 personalized notes to public Shopify and Amazon seller contacts.
- About 10 ecommerce agencies, asking for a warm introduction.
- Seller-community answers that stay on the catalog question. No bulk posting.
- Personal asks for introductions.

Offer on the table, still a hypothesis: 50% off the first 3 months in exchange for structured feedback calls.
