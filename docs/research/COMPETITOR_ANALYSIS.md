## 3. Research checklist (per competitor)

For each selected competitor, capture:

1. Target customer (SMB vs enterprise)  
2. Primary wedge (PIM, feed, AI copy, Shopify-only)  
3. Import/export formats claimed  
4. Whether marketplace **API** sync is claimed  
5. Pricing model (if public) — quote source + date  
6. Notable gaps vs CatalogPilot intended wedge  
7. Review sentiment themes (support, bugs, learning curve) — anecdotal only  

Store notes in dated files or a research sheet; update this doc with **sourced** summaries only.

---

## 4. Working differentiation hypotheses

**[Hypothesis]** CatalogPilot wins when:

1. Merchant needs **pre-publish data ops** across channels without enterprise PIM.  
2. Trust requires **preview, validation scores, and auditability**.  
3. AI is optional assist with review — not the product.  

**[Hypothesis]** CatalogPilot loses when:

1. Merchant is Shopify-only and happy with a $10–20 bulk app.  
2. Enterprise needs MDM workflows / SSO day one.  
3. Merchant expects fully automated Amazon category compliance.
